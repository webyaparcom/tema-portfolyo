FAQ

Questions about packages

The most common questions before a session.

Who holds the rights to the photographs?

Copyright stays with the photographer; each package includes a personal licence and, for product shoots, a commercial licence. Extended licences are discussed separately.

Do you deliver retouched and raw files?

Packages include retouched JPEG files. Raw files are provided only under a separate agreement.

Can a session be cancelled or rescheduled?

Just let us know at least 48 hours ahead; outdoor shoots affected by weather are rescheduled free of charge.

Are these prices real?

No. This is a demo site; prices and packages are fictional.
